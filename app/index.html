Fair while matches are on

Trading pauses during a match

When a player walks on court, their token pauses. Orders placed meanwhile are queued and filled at the price after the final point.

Backed by a funded pool

The pool adds CHZ behind each token as its price rises, and buys are never priced above what a token's reserve holds.
