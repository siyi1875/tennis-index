Methodology. Every match is a zero-sum transfer sized by surprise: pre-match ratings give an expected win probability, the winner takes 200 × (1 − p) × tier × round and the loser gives the same. A favourite's expected win barely moves the level; an upset moves it a lot. Set and game margin adds a small zero-sum adjustment; milestones are surprise-scaled and are the only new points. Every level change is retraceable to its matches.
Reserve model. Tokens mint/burn at NAV = reserve/supply; the core tops up rising NAVs and sweeps falling ones, so tokens stay fully CHZ-backed. Trading fees (1%/1%) fund the core reserve.
